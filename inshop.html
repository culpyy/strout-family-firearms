NFA Builds

The ATF Transfer Process

For NFA items - machine guns, suppressors, short-barreled rifles, and other regulated builds - federal law requires ATF approval before the firearm can leave our license and move to yours or your local FFL. Here's the full process so you know exactly where you stand.

Step 01

Build & Range Check

We complete the build, verify timing and headspace, then run it on the range. You get a finished, fully functioning firearm before any paperwork is filed.

Step 02

ATF Form Filed

We submit the appropriate ATF form - typically Form 3 to move to a receiving FFL, or Form 4 for individual transfers. We handle this on our end. We'll let you know when it goes in.

Step 03

ATF Review Period

ATF reviews and approves the transfer. eForms typically clear in 2 to 8 weeks. Paper forms can take several months. We'll reach out the moment anything comes back from ATF.

Step 04

Approved & Transferred

Once ATF approves the transfer, we ship the item to your FFL or arrange pickup. You take possession through your dealer just like any other NFA transfer.

What you need to do during ATF review: Nothing. We manage the paperwork and the wait. We'll contact you if ATF comes back with any questions, and we'll call you when approval lands. If you have questions about timing on your specific build, give us a call at (207) 332-6999.

Why It Matters

What FFL 07 + SOT Means

Most gunsmiths hold an FFL 01. We hold an FFL 07 and an SOT Tax Stamp. That's a different license class entirely.

An FFL 01 licenses a dealer to sell. An FFL 07 licenses a manufacturer to build, modify, and manufacture firearms commercially. Add the SOT Tax Stamp and we're authorized to manufacture and transfer NFA items, including suppressors, short-barreled rifles, and machine guns.

That's why we can take on work other shops in southern Maine won't touch. Machine gun restorations, Form 1 builds, suppressor manufacturing, Class III transfers - all of it is within our license scope.

Combined with 55+ years of combined shooting experience and a private 80-yard range on the property, we can do the full job from bench to function check without leaving the shop.
