About Us

Family Built,
Expertise Driven

Strout Family Firearms is Southern Maine's trusted source for custom firearm builds, gunsmithing, and certified defensive training. Based out of Limington, ME, we operate as a licensed FFL 07 Manufacturer with a SOT Tax Stamp — giving us the ability to handle everything from standard transfers to NFA items.

Our two instructors bring over five and a half decades of combined shooting experience to every build and every class. Kevin is a certified USCCA Instructor committed to helping shooters at every level build safe, confident fundamentals.
