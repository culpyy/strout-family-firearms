What We Do

Our Services

Custom builds, gunsmithing, training, transfers. We cover it all out of Limington.

01

Custom Builds

Built to your specs from the ground up. Every build gets a proper function check on our range before it leaves. If it doesn't run right, it doesn't go out.

02

Gunsmithing

Trigger work, barrel work, full restorations. Bring us the problem and we'll tell you straight what it needs and what it'll cost.

03

Firearms Training

Kevin is a USCCA-certified instructor. Courses cover draw stroke, low-light shooting, and live-fire drills on our private range. Not just a PowerPoint and a handshake.

04

FFL Transfers

$20 per transfer, in and out of state. Give us a call before you ship anything our way so we can make sure everything is ready on our end.

05

Buy, Sell & Trade

We buy, sell, and trade firearms, gear, and ammo. Fair prices and honest assessments. Bring what you have and we'll give you a real number.

06

Class III / NFA

We hold an FFL 07 and SOT Tax Stamp. NFA items, Class III transfers, all of it. Give us a call if you have questions.

About Us

Family Built,
Unmatched Craft

We're not a big-box dealer. Strout Family Firearms is a licensed FFL 07 Manufacturer and SOT holder out of Limington, Maine. We take on the builds and restorations that need real knowledge behind them. Custom rifles, historic machine gun work, the stuff most shops won't touch.

Our two instructors have over 55 years of combined shooting experience between them. Kevin teaches defensive shooting the right way. You're learning to use a firearm under pressure, not just stand at a line and pull a trigger. That experience carries into every build we do.
