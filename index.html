About Us

Family Built,
Unmatched Craft

Strout Family Firearms isn't a big-box dealer slapping parts together. We're a licensed FFL 07 Manufacturer and SOT holder operating out of Limington, Maine — taking on the builds and restorations that demand real knowledge and real craftsmanship. From ground-up custom rifles to rare historic machine gun rebuilds, we tackle work others won't touch.

Our two instructors carry over five and a half decades of combined trigger time into every build and every class. Kevin is a USCCA-certified instructor who teaches shooters to move, think, and operate — not just punch paper. When you come to us, you get that depth of experience behind every decision we make.
